Local Transportation

Banff is 130 km west of Calgary International Airport (YYC) on the Trans-Canada Highway, about 90 minutes to two hours by road. There is no train service.

Banff Airporter shuttle: 15% off for participants

ICNC 2027 is pleased to partner with Banff Airporter to offer participants a convenient way to travel between Calgary International Airport and Banff Centre. Banff Airporter operates 14 scheduled departures daily in each direction, so you can choose the shuttle that best fits your flight schedule. Service in Banff is door to door, so there is no need to rent a vehicle.

Promo code: ICNC2027
15% off Banff Airporter’s scheduled shuttle service between Calgary International Airport and Banff.
Valid for travel from 29 May to 7 June 2027. Bookings with the code can be made now.

How to book

  1. Go to banffairporter.com and select Book Now.
  2. Enter your travel details, with Banff Centre as your Banff address.
  3. On the final payment page, enter ICNC2027 in the “Promo Code” field to apply the discount.

To guarantee your seat, book at least 2–3 weeks in advance. Cancellations are free up to one hour before pickup, and if a flight delay makes you miss your shuttle you are placed on the next one.

Around Banff

Roam Transit local buses stop at Banff Centre and connect it with downtown Banff, the Banff Gondola and the Upper Hot Springs; see roamtransit.com for routes and fares. Taxis are available in town. Downtown is a 20-minute walk downhill from the campus.

Time zone

Banff is on Mountain Daylight Time (UTC−6) in June.

ICNC 2027

21st International Conference on Numerical Combustion
Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity, 107 Tunnel Mountain Drive, Banff, Alberta, Canada
Tuesday 1 to Friday 4 June 2027

Banff Centre is located in Treaty 7 territory, the traditional lands of the Îyârhe Nakoda (Stoney Nakoda) Bearspaw, Chiniki and Goodstoney Nations, the Tsuut’ina First Nation and the Blackfoot Confederacy (Siksika, Piikani, Kainai), and home to the Métis Nation of Alberta.

The campus

Banff Centre has hosted conferences for decades and is a member of the International Association of Conference Centres. The Kinnear Centre, where the conference takes place, is a LEED-certified building with daylight in every meeting room and views of the Bow Valley. Guests staying on campus have access to the Sally Borden fitness and recreation centre (pool, climbing gym, running track), the Walter Phillips Gallery and the campus library.

Accessibility

The Kinnear Centre and the campus hotels are wheelchair accessible. If you have accessibility or dietary requirements, tell us during registration or write to the organizers.

Land acknowledgement

Banff Centre is located in Treaty 7 territory, the traditional lands of the Îyârhe Nakoda (Stoney Nakoda) Bearspaw, Chiniki and Goodstoney Nations, the Tsuut’ina First Nation and the Blackfoot Confederacy (Siksika, Piikani, Kainai), and home to the Métis Nation of Alberta. We are grateful to gather on this land.

Banff is 130 km west of Calgary International Airport (YYC) on the Trans-Canada Highway, about 90 minutes to two hours by road. There is no train service.

Around Banff

Roam Transit local buses stop at Banff Centre and connect it with downtown Banff, the Banff Gondola and the Upper Hot Springs; see roamtransit.com for routes and fares. Taxis are available in town. Downtown is a 20-minute walk downhill from the campus.

Time zone

Banff is on Mountain Daylight Time (UTC−6) in June.

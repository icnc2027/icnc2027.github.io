Meals included in the registration fee are served on campus. For other meals there are two restaurants on campus and many more in the town of Banff.

On campus

  • Vistas Dining Room: market-style buffet with mountain views (conference meals are served here).
  • Maclab Bistro: burgers, flatbreads, craft beer and a large patio.

In town

Banff Avenue and the streets around it, a 20-minute walk from the campus, offer everything from quick lunches to fine dining, plus pubs, breweries, bakeries and cafés. The tourism board keeps an up-to-date directory at banfflakelouise.com. Reservations are strongly recommended for dinner in June.

A list of restaurants suggested by the local organizers will be added here before the conference.

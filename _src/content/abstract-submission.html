Abstract submission opens on 1 November 2026 and closes on 31 January 2027. Authors will be notified by 15 February 2027. The submission link will appear on this page.

What to expect

  • A short text abstract in English; the length limit and template will be announced.
  • Title, all authors and affiliations, and the presenting author.
  • One topic or one mini-symposium for the talk.

All presentations are oral. No full paper is requested and no proceedings are published.

After submission

Abstracts are reviewed by the scientific committee and authors are notified by 15 February 2027. To keep a talk in the program, the presenting author must register for the conference.

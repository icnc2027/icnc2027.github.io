What to expect

  • A short text abstract in English; the length limit and template will be announced.
  • Title, all authors and affiliations, and the presenting author.
  • One topic or one mini-symposium for the talk.

All presentations are oral. No full paper is requested and no proceedings are published.

After submission

Abstracts are reviewed by the scientific committee. To keep a talk in the program, the presenting author must register by the registration deadline for selected contributors (see important dates).

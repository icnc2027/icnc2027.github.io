Abstract submission opens on 1 November 2026 and closes on 31 January 2027. Authors will be notified by 15 February 2027. The submission link will appear on this page.

What to expect

  • A one-page abstract in English (US Letter, including figures and references), prepared with the template below and uploaded as a PDF.
  • Title, all authors and affiliations, and the presenting author.
  • One topic or one mini-symposium for the talk.

All presentations are oral. No full paper is requested and no proceedings are published. Accepted abstracts are printed in the Book of Abstracts.

Abstract template

Use one of the templates below and keep its page size (US Letter), margins and font sizes. The complete abstract — title, authors, affiliations, any figures and references — must fit on one page. The LaTeX package can be uploaded to Overleaf as it is (set main.tex as the main document, pdfLaTeX).

Mini-symposium talks

Mini-symposium talks are submitted through the same form, by invitation of the organizers only, by 28 February 2027. The mini-symposium list appears in the form as proposals are accepted. Mini-symposium speakers are notified by 7 March 2027.

After submission

Abstracts are reviewed by the scientific committee and authors are notified by 15 February 2027. To keep a talk in the program, the presenting author must register for the conference.

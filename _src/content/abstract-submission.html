Abstract submission opens on 15 December 2026 and closes on 7 February 2027. Authors will be notified by 1 March 2027. The submission link will appear on this page.

What to expect

  • A short text abstract in English; the length limit and template will be announced.
  • Title, all authors and affiliations, and the presenting author.
  • One topic or one mini-symposium for the talk. Only speakers invited by the organizers of a mini-symposium may submit to it.

All presentations are oral. No full paper is requested and no proceedings are published.

After submission

Abstracts are reviewed by the scientific committee and authors are notified by 1 March 2027. To keep a talk in the program, the presenting author must register for the conference.

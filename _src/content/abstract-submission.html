Submission system opens 2 November 2026

What to submit

  • A text abstract of up to 200 words (no figures, no references), in English.
  • Title, all authors and affiliations, and the presenting author.
  • One topic or one mini-symposium for the talk.

All presentations are oral, 20 minutes including discussion. No full paper is requested and no proceedings are published. One presenting author may present at most two contributions.

After submission

Abstracts are reviewed by the scientific committee; authors are notified by 22 January 2027 tentative. To keep a talk in the program, the presenting author must register by 8 February 2027 tentative.

Guidance for oral presentations. Details are tentative and will be confirmed with the final program.

Contributed talks

  • Each slot is 20 minutes: plan for a 15-minute talk and 5 minutes of discussion. Session chairs keep strictly to time so that participants can move between parallel sessions.
  • Every session room has a laptop, projector and screen. Bring your presentation on a USB drive as a PDF or PowerPoint file (16:9) and load it during the break before your session. tentative
  • Please introduce yourself to the session chair before the session starts.

Invited lectures

Invited lectures are 40 minutes including discussion, in the Cenovus Great Hall.

Language

The conference language is English.

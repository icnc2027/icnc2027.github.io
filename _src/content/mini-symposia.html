Mini-symposia gather several talks on a focused theme, organised by one or more colleagues who recruit the speakers and chair the sessions.

Call for mini-symposium proposals to be announced

A call for proposals, with the submission procedure and deadline, will be published on this page. Accepted mini-symposia will be listed here and selectable in the abstract submission form. Speakers in a mini-symposium submit their abstracts through the same form as contributed talks and register as regular participants.

Mini-symposia are sessions on a focused theme, organized and moderated by one or two experts, with 4–6 invited talks in a two-hour session. A second session may be requested for broader themes. Proposals are welcome on any topic within the scope of the conference.

Call for mini-symposium proposals

To propose a mini-symposium, email the program chair (xiaohang.fang@ucalgary.ca) by 1 December 2026 with:

  • title of the mini-symposium;
  • organizer(s): name, affiliation, email (one or two);
  • scope, up to 250 words (printed in the Book of Abstracts);
  • planned speakers (4–6) with tentative talk titles;
  • number of sessions requested (one or two).

Decisions will be sent by 15 December 2026, and accepted mini-symposia will be listed on this page.

Speakers in a mini-symposium

Speakers in an accepted mini-symposium submit their abstracts through the regular abstract submission form between 15 December 2026 and 7 February 2027, selecting the mini-symposium from the list. Only speakers invited by the organizers may submit to a mini-symposium. All speakers, including organizers, register as regular participants.

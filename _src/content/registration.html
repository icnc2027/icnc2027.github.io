What the registration fee includes

  • attendance at all scientific sessions;
  • breakfast, lunch and coffee breaks on campus on 2, 3 and 4 June;
  • the welcome reception on Tuesday 1 June;
  • the conference banquet;
  • the early-career researcher mixer and the Women in Combustion lunch;
  • the online book of abstracts.

The excursion is optional and costs USD 150 per person; it can be added during registration. Accompanying persons are welcome at the welcome reception and the banquet.

At registration

You will be asked for the name to be printed on your badge and for any dietary requirements (vegetarian or vegan, gluten-free, halal or kosher, food allergies).

Cancellations and refunds

Refunds are subject to a 20% administration fee and are available up to 7 days before the start of the conference. No refunds are made within 7 days of the start of the conference.

Visa invitation letters

Invitation letters for visa applications are issued on request to participants whose registration is complete and paid. See the visa page.

What the registration fee will include

Details will be confirmed with the fees. As at previous ICNC meetings, the fee is expected to cover attendance at all scientific sessions, meals and coffee breaks on session days, the welcome reception and the online book of abstracts. Payment, invoices and cancellations

Payment will be by credit card in Canadian dollars through the online registration system, with an automatic receipt. The cancellation policy will be published together with the fees.

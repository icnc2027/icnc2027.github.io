Tourist Information

Banff is Canada’s oldest national park and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Early June brings long daylight (sunset after 21:30), open valley trails and few crowds compared with July and August. Consider arriving early or staying on.

Within the town of Banff

  • Banff Gondola and Sulphur Mountain: eight minutes to the summit boardwalk.
  • Banff Upper Hot Springs: outdoor hot pool next to the gondola base.
  • Cave and Basin National Historic Site: the thermal springs where the park began in 1885.
  • Bow Falls, the Fenland and Tunnel Mountain trails: short walks starting in town; Tunnel Mountain starts right beside Banff Centre.
  • Whyte Museum of the Canadian Rockies and the Banff Park Museum.

Day trips

  • Lake Louise and Moraine Lake (60 km): access to Moraine Lake is by Parks Canada shuttle or tour bus only, and shuttles must be reserved in advance; Moraine Lake Road normally opens in late May or early June.
  • Lake Minnewanka and Johnston Canyon: lakeside drives and a canyon boardwalk close to town.
  • Icefields Parkway towards Jasper: one of the world’s great mountain roads.

Practical notes

  • A Parks Canada entry pass is required for the park (day or annual Discovery Pass); hotel guests arriving by shuttle still need one to visit park sites.
  • Weather in early June ranges from about 5 to 20 °C; bring layers, a rain jacket and sturdy shoes. Higher trails may still hold snow.
  • Wildlife, including bears and elk, is common around town: keep your distance and follow park guidance.
  • Official visitor information: banfflakelouise.com and parks.canada.ca.

21st International Conference on Numerical Combustion
Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity, 107 Tunnel Mountain Drive, Banff, Alberta, Canada
Tuesday 1 to Friday 4 June 2027

Banff Centre is located in Treaty 7 territory, the traditional lands of the Îyârhe Nakoda (Stoney Nakoda) Bearspaw, Chiniki and Goodstoney Nations, the Tsuut’ina First Nation and the Blackfoot Confederacy (Siksika, Piikani, Kainai), and home to the Métis Nation of Alberta.

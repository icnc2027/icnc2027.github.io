ICNC 2027 / Information

Hotels

A block of rooms is being reserved for participants at Banff Centre, on the conference campus. June is the busiest month of the year in Banff: plan to book early.

On campus: Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity

Conference room rates, the booking procedure and the booking deadline will be published here. to be announced

Rooms are a short walk from the Kinnear Centre where the sessions take place. Rates will exclude applicable taxes and a nightly facility fee that covers Wi-Fi, parking and the campus fitness and recreation facilities.

In the town of Banff

Hotels in town are about 15 to 30 minutes on foot from Banff Centre, or a short bus or taxi ride. Examples, closest first:

Budget options include the HI Banff Alpine Centre hostel and the YWCA Banff Hotel. Participants staying off campus are welcome at all meals and coffee breaks included in the registration fee.

ICNC 2027

21st International Conference on Numerical Combustion
Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity, 107 Tunnel Mountain Drive, Banff, Alberta, Canada
Tuesday 1 to Friday 4 June 2027

Banff Centre is located in Treaty 7 territory, the traditional lands of the Îyârhe Nakoda (Stoney Nakoda) Bearspaw, Chiniki and Goodstoney Nations, the Tsuut’ina First Nation and the Blackfoot Confederacy (Siksika, Piikani, Kainai), and home to the Métis Nation of Alberta.

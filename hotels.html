Hotels

A block of rooms is reserved for participants at Banff Centre, on the conference campus, for the nights of 1, 2 and 3 June 2027. June is the busiest month of the year in Banff: book early.

On campus: Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity

Room categoryRate per night (single or double occupancy)
Run of houseCAD 349
Two-bedded roomCAD 349
Superior king plusCAD 389
Superior executive suiteCAD 429
Superior one-bedroom suite / Lloyd Hall studioCAD 469

Rates exclude 2% tourism improvement fee, 4% Alberta tourism levy, 5% GST, and a facility fee of CAD 20 per room per night (covers Wi-Fi, parking, the Sally Borden fitness and recreation facilities and campus arts events). The same rates apply for up to three nights before and after the conference, subject to availability.

How to book
Online booking link: to be published here. By phone: Banff Centre Reservations, +1 800 884 7574 (mention “International Conference on Numerical Combustion 2027”).
Booking deadline
4 May 2027. After this date the remaining rooms are released and the conference rate is no longer guaranteed.
Check-in / check-out
16:00 / 11:00
Sharing
Two-bedded rooms can be shared by two participants at the same rate; a third or fourth adult in a room costs CAD 20 per person per night.

Banff Centre is located in Treaty 7 territory, the traditional lands of the Îyârhe Nakoda (Stoney Nakoda) Bearspaw, Chiniki and Goodstoney Nations, the Tsuut’ina First Nation and the Blackfoot Confederacy (Siksika, Piikani, Kainai), and home to the Métis Nation of Alberta.
